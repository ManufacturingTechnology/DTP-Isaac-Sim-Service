# Research

**Finished research, promoted out of `Scratch/`.** Research starts in the user's local, gitignored `Scratch/`
(ADR-0017), where it is worked on with the user. When a body of research is complete and reviewed, it is
**promoted here**, keeping its file names and dates, and committed.

Promoted reports are dated, cited, and **immutable**: corrections and updates go in a new report that
references the old one. (Path or link fixes and privacy redactions are the only permitted edits, recorded
with a front-matter `note:`.)
